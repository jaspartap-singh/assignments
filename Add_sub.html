A scientist modeled the time (in sec) required by two substances to completely dissolve in water as:

  • \( t_1 = \frac{x + 5}{x - 5} \) and
  • \( t_2 = \frac{5x}{x + 5} \) (Where \(x\) is a real number greater than \(5\).)

Now, suppose that these two substances are dissolved in water one at a time.

So, the total time (in sec) required by the two substances to completely dissolve in water will be written as:

\( \frac{6x^2 + 15x + 25}{x^2 - 25} \)
\( \frac{6x^2 - 15x + 25}{x^2 - 25} \)
\( \frac{25x + 25}{(x + 5)(x - 5)} \)
\( \frac{6x^2 - 15x + 25}{x^2 + 25} \)
\( \frac{6x^2 - 15x + 25}{(x + 5)^2} \)
