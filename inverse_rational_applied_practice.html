A large pipe can fill a tank in \( 9\text{ hours} \). A smaller pipe can fill the same tank in \( t\text{ hours} \). When both pipes run together, the tank is full in \( 6\text{ hours} \).

a. Which rational equation in \(t\) correctly models this situation?
\( \frac{1}{9} + \frac{1}{t} = \frac{1}{6} \)
\( \frac{1}{9} - \frac{1}{t} = \frac{1}{6} \)
\( 9 + t = 6 \)
\( \frac{9}{t} = \frac{1}{6} \)

A wildlife biologist models the concentration \( C \) (in mg/L) of a sedative in a tranquilizer dart as a function of the volume \( v \) (in mL) of solution used:

$$ C(v) = \frac{120}{v - 4}, \quad v > 4 $$
a. Find the inverse function \( v(C) \).
\( v(C) = \frac{120}{C} + 4 \)
\( v(C) = \frac{120}{C} - 4 \)
\( v(C) = \frac{C - 4}{120} \)
\( v(C) = \frac{120}{C + 4} \)

\( v(15) = 12\text{ mL} \)
\( v(15) = 8\text{ mL} \)
\( v(15) = 16\text{ mL} \)
\( v(15) = 10\text{ mL} \)
