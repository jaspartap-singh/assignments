A student used synthetic division to divide \( 2x^3 + 5x - 3 \) by \( x - 2 \), setting it up as shown:

2 2 5 -3
4 18
2 9 15

From the bottom row they read off a quotient of \( 2x + 9 \) and a remainder of \( 15 \). What error did the student make?

The dividend \( 2x^3 + 5x - 3 \) has no \( x^2 \) term, so the top row should be \( 2, 0, 5, -3 \) — a \( 0 \) must be inserted for the missing term.
The last number, \( 15 \), is part of the quotient, not the remainder.
Each new entry should be added to the divisor rather than multiplied by it.
The number to the left of the bar should be \( -2 \) instead of \( 2 \), since the divisor is \( x - 2 \).
